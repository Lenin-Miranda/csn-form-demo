# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful place to collaborate. Everyone participating is expected to communicate with care, assume good intent, and help keep the project useful for students, staff, and developers.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Use clear language and explain context when possible.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive student or credential data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that does not fit these expectations, open a private conversation with the repository maintainer or project lead. Reports should include what happened, when it happened, and any relevant links or screenshots.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, revoke access, or take other action needed to protect the project.
